AIn progress · 2026

VQ-VAE + Conformer Translation System

Continuous sign language translation with hierarchical quantization and sequence modeling
VQ-VAE Conformer CTC-Attention Beam Search PyTorch MediaPipe
Key contributions
01 Designing a factorized VQ-VAE that discretizes 3D landmark sequences into 4 hierarchical factors (Pose, Motion, Dynamics, Facial Expressions), aiming to reduce latent-space complexity for translation.
02 Building a Conformer Seq2Seq model with a hybrid CTC + attention decoder and beam search with CTC prefix scoring, to align visual tokens with glosses. Training and evaluation in progress.
03 Implemented hand-dominance normalization and body-relative coordinate preprocessing, so evaluation can be signer-independent and robust to handedness and camera position.
AST-GCN · 2025

Graph-Based Sign Recognition

Graph-network baseline over 209 skeletal joints, outperformed by the LandmarkConformer
ST-GCNPyTorchGraph NetworksMediaPipe
Key contributions
01 ST-GCN modeling 209 skeletal joints as a dynamic graph; D⁻¹/²AD⁻¹/² normalization on a custom adjacency matrix for stable feature aggregation.
02 Edge Importance Weighting + Spatial Attention to dynamically prioritize key physiological connections (e.g., fingertips) during high-velocity signs.
03 Residual Graph Blocks with Multi-Scale Temporal Kernels capturing motion from rapid finger movements to full postural transitions.
AConformer · 2024

LandmarkConformer

Multi-stream Conformer (convolution + self-attention) over body landmarks · Google ASL Signs, 250 classes
PyTorchConformerGradient ReversalMediaPipe
Key contributions
01 Three fused streams from 131 of MediaPipe’s 543 landmarks (hands, pose, eyebrows, mouth): body-relative positions, multi-scale velocities, and hand geometry (joint angles, fingertip distances, palm direction), fed to 4 Conformer blocks.
02 76.65% top-1 (76.69% with 5-pass TTA) across 250 signs, on a split by signer, so no validation signer appears in training.
03 Signer invariance from hand-dominance mirroring, shoulder-width scaling and an adversarial signer discriminator (gradient reversal); Mixup, finger dropout and focal loss for regularisation.
